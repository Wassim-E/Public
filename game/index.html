Rules
  • Each player places 8 edges on their half (top / bottom).
  • An edge is a unit piece on a square side or a square diagonal.
  • Player 1 places first, hits Ready, then hands the phone to Player 2 (P1's edges stay hidden).
  • On your turn, move 2 edges. A move pivots an edge around one of its endpoints to any other valid unit edge (size 1).
  • Landing on an opponent edge captures it. You cannot land on your own edge.
  • End game ⇒ player with the most edges wins.
