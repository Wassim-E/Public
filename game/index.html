Rules
  • Each player places 4 edges on their half (top / bottom) of a 6×6 grid.
  • An edge is a unit piece on a square side or a square diagonal.
  • Player 1 places first, hits Ready, then hands the phone to Player 2 (P1's edges stay hidden).
  • At the start of each turn, roll a die (1–6) — that's how many moves you get this turn.
  • A move pivots an edge around one of its endpoints to any other valid unit edge (size 1). You may move the same edge multiple times.
  • Landing on an opponent edge captures it. You cannot land on your own edge.
  • End game ⇒ player with the most edges wins.
